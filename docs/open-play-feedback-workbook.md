## Part 4 — On the matchmaking changes we're testing

We looked at a recent Numbering session and found people were getting repeat **opponents**
(a few faced the same person 3–4 times). We're planning to change Numbering so it:

- **remembers** recent partners/opponents and avoids repeating them,
- still **prioritizes** whoever's waited longest (fairly, without punishing early arrivers),
- generates the **next match just as a court opens** instead of pre-booking several ahead.
