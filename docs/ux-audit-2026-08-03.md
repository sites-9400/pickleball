# UX / UI Audit — Paddle District organizer app

**Date:** 2026-08-03
**By:** Claude (Opus 4.8), after operating the live app end-to-end
**Method:** Drove the real `app.html` in a browser (Firebase stubbed) through the full
organizer flow — session start → add players → attendance → courts → queue → rankings →
share/QR — during the Numbering mode test.
**Caveat:** Desktop viewport with a simulated session; not every modal (score-submit, swap)
was tapped through on a real phone. Treat touch-target notes as flags to verify on-device.

Pair this with Jude's answers in the **feedback workbook** (Google Doc + `docs/open-play-feedback-workbook.md`) — where her notes and an item here point at the same thing, that's a strong signal to prioritize.

---

## High impact

### 1. Destructive action sits directly under the primary one
On each court card, **"Cancel match"** (red, full-width) is immediately beneath **"Submit
Score"** (green, full-width), at the same size. A mis-tap mid-game can discard a real match.
- **Recommend:** demote Cancel to a smaller/tertiary text link, or move it behind the
  court's "×"/overflow menu. Highest-risk item observed.
- **Cross-check with Jude:** ask if she's ever cancelled a match by accident.

### 2. The Courts tab doesn't show who's up next
On-deck depth is now 1 (Numbering), but to see the next match the organizer must leave
Courts for the Queue tab. "Who's next" is the thing an organizer glances at between games.
- **Recommend:** surface the single "Next up" foursome on the Courts tab (slim card under
  the courts). Directly reduces manual queue-fiddling.
- **Cross-check with Jude:** workbook Part 2 ("confusing to see who's up next").

### 3. Rankings + full game history on one endless page
The full game-history list (76+ rows over a night) stacks under the standings — a very long
scroll to check standings mid-session.
- **Recommend:** collapse game history behind a "Show game history" toggle, or lazy-render;
  keep standings above the fold.

---

## Medium

### 4. Setup cards never get out of the way
The Players tab stacks Share link, Check-in link, Co-host panel, Add Players, *then*
attendance — all visible all night, though most are setup-time concerns.
- **Recommend:** group Share / Check-in / Co-host into one "Session setup ▾" section that
  auto-collapses once play starts.

### 5. Small tap targets for swaps
The ⇄ arrows beside each on-court player are tiny; easy to mis-tap on a phone.
- **Recommend:** enlarge hit area (≥44px), or move swap into a per-player tap menu.

### 6. Low-contrast micro-labels
"SCORE", "INT", "MANUAL" render as light-gray small caps — borderline for outdoor daytime
phone use.
- **Recommend:** bump contrast; quick WCAG check on the olive palette.

---

## Low / polish

7. **"INT" skill abbreviation** isn't obvious to a first-time organizer → tooltip or full word on first use.
8. **Empty states are good** ("No players yet. Add some above!") → extend the same friendly nudge to an empty Courts tab.
9. **Header (timer + "N playing")** is clear and useful → no change.

---

## Suggested sequencing
Ship **#1 and #2** first — small, high-value changes that cut live-session friction and
mis-taps. Then #3–#6 as a follow-up polish pass. Keep this separate from the matchmaking PR
so reviews stay clean.
